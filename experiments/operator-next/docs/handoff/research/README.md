The current UX synthesis is ../ux-research.md; current adoption decisions are ../decisions.md. Do not collapse them into a claim that ChatGPT/Gemini uses A2UI or that a framework supplies data correctness/authorization. Public primary links are retained. Raw web/PDF body captures and temporary browser/review artifacts are not needed to continue the app and are not bundled. Relative historical context links may name the original ignored research packet; they are provenance, not current dependencies.
